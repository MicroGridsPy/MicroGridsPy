# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the MicroGridsPy community to be a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate in language and actions.
- Welcoming newcomers and offering constructive feedback.
- Gracefully accepting differing viewpoints and criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers via the [issue tracker](https://github.com/MicroGridsPy/MicroGridsPy/issues)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Maintainers will review and respond to reports in
a way they deem appropriate, and will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.

> This is a minimal Code of Conduct. It is adapted in spirit from the
> [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), v2.1, and may be
> replaced by the full text in a future revision.
